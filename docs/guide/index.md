Zero is a Mac app for coding agents. It runs Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, each signed in with your own account. You give an agent a job and a folder to work in. When it needs you, it asks in the inbox, where every agent asks and you answer.

## The parts of Zero

- **The window.** Your tasks and their conversations. The rail on its left side leads to Home, the Inbox, Later, Scratchpads, Loops and Settings.
- **The control bar.** A small bar on the right edge of your screen. It shows the inbox and a mark for each task. It keeps your agents in view while you work in another app.
- **The inbox.** Everything your agents have asked you: questions, choices, commands to allow. It opens beside the control bar, and it's also a page in the window.
- **The iPhone app.** Your inbox and tasks on your phone, coming soon.

## Words these docs use

- **Task**: one agent that works on one job, in one folder. Each task has a conversation, like a chat.
- **Worker**: a task that another task started for part of its job. It shows under the task that started it, and it reports back to that task.
- **Ask**: something an agent needs from you, such as an answer, a choice or your go-ahead.
- **Account**: a sign-in for Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode that Zero keeps and gives to the agent.
- **Project**: a folder on your Mac that you added to Zero. Tasks start in a project.
- **Workspace**: a set of tasks kept apart from the rest, such as your own projects and your job. Each task belongs to one workspace.
